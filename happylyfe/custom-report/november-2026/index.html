Conversion tracking

What is in place

  • GA4 tracking is fixed. It records the full shopper journey from the first visit to purchase.
  • We treat GA4 conversions as our Google Ads results.
  • We are pushing the GA4 conversions from the Cross-network and Paid Search channels into Google Ads.

Why it matters

  • Google Ads learns from real sales and finds more buyers like them.
  • Not every conversion reaches Google Ads yet, so GA4 is our reference for sales today.
  • The remarketing campaign was added recently, so it feeds the next report.
